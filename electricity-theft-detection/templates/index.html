Smart Electricity Theft Detection System

A machine learning system that studies electricity consumption patterns and flags records that look suspicious, so inspectors can focus on the cases most worth checking.

Start Analyzing Consumption →

Why it matters

Electricity theft (meter tampering, bypassing, illegal connections) causes large revenue loss for utilities, overloads the grid and raises costs for honest consumers.

How it works

Consumption data is turned into features such as monthly change, variability and zero-reading days. Four classifiers are trained and compared; the best one powers the predictions.

What it does not do

A "suspicious" result is a lead for investigation, not proof of theft. Genuine holidays, outages or vacant premises can look similar.
